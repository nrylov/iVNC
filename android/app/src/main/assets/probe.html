Connection

High Performance creates a virtual display and uses software HEVC decoding. It falls back to Standard if negotiation fails. Audio is not included in this build.

Saved login details are encrypted with Android Keystore.

Slide anywhere on the desktop to move the pointer. Tap to click; two-finger tap to right-click. Move two fingers together to scroll, or pinch to zoom.
